AUTOMATIC SCENE SETUP - Quick Start
====================================

This is the EASIEST way to set up your dark chamber scene. Just follow these 4 steps:

STEP 1: Open Your Scene
-----------------------
- Open your Level01 scene in Unity

STEP 2: Create Setup GameObject
--------------------------------
- Right-click in Hierarchy
- Select: Create Empty
- Name it: "SceneSetup"

STEP 3: Add AutoSceneSetup Script
---------------------------------
- Select the "SceneSetup" GameObject
- In Inspector, click "Add Component"
- Search for: "AutoSceneSetup"
- Click to add it

STEP 4: Play the Scene
----------------------
- Press Play button (or Ctrl+P / Cmd+P)
- Watch the console output
- When you see "✓ Scene setup complete!" - you're done!

WHAT GETS CREATED AUTOMATICALLY
================================
✓ Dark chamber geometry (floor, walls, ceiling, windows)
✓ Realistic lighting (dark blue ambient + directional light)
✓ Physics tuning for blob movement
✓ Blob merge system
✓ Camera positioned correctly

CONTROLS
========
- WASD: Move blobs
- SPACE: Jump
- TAB: Switch control mode (Both / Blob 1 / Blob 2)
- X: Separate merged blobs

CUSTOMIZATION
=============
In the Inspector (AutoSceneSetup component):
- Disable any step you don't want (e.g., uncheck "createChamber" if you have your own geometry)
- Adjust "setupDelay" if needed (default 0.1s)

DONE!
=====
Your scene is fully configured and ready to play. All materials, physics, lighting, 
and gameplay mechanics are automatically set up to match the dark chamber design.
